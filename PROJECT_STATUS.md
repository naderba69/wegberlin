## Honest limitations
- Evidence scores are internal training indicators, not CEFR placement or official exam points. Reading/listening/grammar scores combine latest-attempt accuracy and coverage; writing/speaking indicators mainly measure quantity, revision, duration, repetition, and a small self-rating component, not human-rated linguistic quality.
- Per-lesson completion now requires controlled, reading, listening, and Mini-Test evidence. It does not require a writing and speaking submission in every single lesson; productive evidence is accumulated and enforced at module/level gates to avoid forcing 84 recordings and 84 texts.
- Error remediation uses deterministic normalized matching against the published correction and explicit alternatives. It cannot judge a novel but semantically equivalent paraphrase like a human teacher, so reveal remains available when the expected form is unclear.
- Reading evidence quotes are always literal source sentences and are hidden until commitment, but sentence selection is token-overlap automation rather than 252 independently authored evidence annotations. A human German reviewer must validate semantic sufficiency before this partial P0 item can be closed.
- The 84-lesson A1–B2 curriculum and the separate original Goethe/telc simulation banks are published, but they are not official provider material or an official certification product.
- All six full simulations for both providers now have guided and continuous-timed modes. The continuous mode has a real absolute central deadline, fresh evidence, expiry, sequence enforcement, reload-safe receptive/writing drafts, and an in-app closed-assistance focus shell that removes normal navigation and withholds keys/transcripts/helpers. It warns before unload and confirms in-app exit/abandonment, but it cannot prevent closing the browser, switching tabs/devices, or using outside resources. A speaking recording is written to temporary IndexedDB media as soon as MediaRecorder stops and can be restored/promoted after reload. Audio can still be lost if the page closes while MediaRecorder is actively capturing, before the stop event produces a Blob. It is not invigilation, kiosk mode, or an official locked exam.
- Keyboard order, focus movement, modal trapping, ARIA state, contrast, and serious/critical axe rules are automated in Chromium desktop/mobile. This does not equal hands-on validation with NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack, Firefox, physical iOS Safari, or switch-control hardware.
- AI provider calls depend on browser CORS, provider availability, the user's endpoint/key, and the provider's current free quota. Per-send consent controls whether this app initiates a request; it cannot control the external provider's retention or terms after sending. OpenRouter is technically restricted to Free-only IDs, while Gemini free-tier availability can change; the app never enables billing or falls back to a paid model.
- `.dwnb` v2/v3 can include and restore audio; optional passphrase mode encrypts the full payload with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (250,000 iterations) and AES-GCM. Merge/Replace/New-profile preview, deterministic conflict handling, and a reversible pre-import restore-point swap are implemented and the encrypted New-profile UI route is browser-tested.
