## Honest limitations
- The public repository `https://github.com/naderba69/wegberlin` is readable. Rechecked on 2026-09-04, its latest `main` commit remains `7c2849b00cacf8ecdd0d031e3db8f453fbf3a776` (`Clean replacement with audited WegBerlin project`, committed 2026-09-02). The local source-governance, academic-audit, mastery/calendar, and A1 lexical-grammar batches are not present there yet: both `src/config/source-verification-registry.json` and `src/data/lexical-grammar-a1.ts` returned 404. A new user-authorized push is required. No Vercel production or PR Preview URL has been verified.
- Evidence scores are internal training indicators, not CEFR placement or official exam points. Reading/listening/grammar scores combine latest-attempt accuracy and coverage; writing/speaking indicators mainly measure quantity, revision, duration, repetition, and a small self-rating component, not human-rated linguistic quality.
- Per-lesson completion now requires controlled, reading, listening, and Mini-Test evidence. It does not require a writing and speaking submission in every single lesson; productive evidence is accumulated and enforced at module/level gates to avoid forcing 84 recordings and 84 texts.
- Error remediation uses deterministic normalized matching against the published correction and explicit alternatives. It cannot judge a novel but semantically equivalent paraphrase like a human teacher, so reveal remains available when the expected form is unclear.
- Reading evidence quotes are always literal source sentences and are hidden until commitment, but sentence selection is token-overlap automation rather than 252 independently authored evidence annotations. A human German reviewer must validate semantic sufficiency before this partial P0 item can be closed.
- Zod proves object shape and cross-reference integrity, not German/Arabic correctness or CEFR validity. The answer audit detects direct normalized answer surfaces with type-aware policies, not every possible semantic hint. The 336-objective report is a lesson-scoped structural map; it proves that teaching/practice/assessment surfaces exist but still needs human item-level semantic alignment review.
- Novelty is determined by stable item ID and lesson stage: a first unseen receptive/Mini-Test item is treated as transfer, not psychometrically calibrated novelty for an objective. Existing scheduled review instants are not retroactively moved when the operating-system timezone changes; future grades record and use the newly resolved browser timezone.
- The lexical grammar panel currently annotates four high-priority noun anchors per lesson plus one verb-preposition frame per A1 lesson and two per A2/B1/B2 lesson. It is authored and schema-checked across all 84 lessons, but it is not an exhaustive lexical database, does not cover every target noun or every verb with a prepositional complement, has no nominal Genitiv or dative-plural forms, and still needs independent German review before being described as final linguistic validation.
- The 84-lesson A1–B2 curriculum and the separate original Goethe/telc simulation banks are published, but they are not official provider material or an official certification product.
- All six full simulations for both providers now have guided and continuous-timed modes. The continuous mode has a real absolute central deadline, fresh evidence, expiry, sequence enforcement, reload-safe receptive/writing drafts, and an in-app closed-assistance focus shell that removes normal navigation and withholds keys/transcripts/helpers. It warns before unload and confirms in-app exit/abandonment, but it cannot prevent closing the browser, switching tabs/devices, or using outside resources. A speaking recording is written to temporary IndexedDB media as soon as MediaRecorder stops and can be restored/promoted after reload. Audio can still be lost if the page closes while MediaRecorder is actively capturing, before the stop event produces a Blob. It is not invigilation, kiosk mode, or an official locked exam.
- Keyboard order, focus movement, modal trapping, ARIA state, contrast, and serious/critical axe rules are automated in Chromium desktop/mobile. This does not equal hands-on validation with NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack, Firefox, physical iOS Safari, or switch-control hardware.
- AI provider calls depend on browser CORS, provider availability, the user's endpoint/key, and the provider's current free quota. Per-send consent controls whether this app initiates a request; it cannot control the external provider's retention or terms after sending. OpenRouter is technically restricted to Free-only IDs, and Gemini is restricted to the currently verified allowlist; both are blocked after 30 days without source review. The app cannot inspect whether a learner linked billing to a Google Cloud project, so Gemini's hard-zero path requires a Free Tier project without billing; otherwise Disabled/Ollama must be used.
