# General consular and legal language-practice claims

Policy: `general-consular-legal-claims-v1`

The registry contains twelve training rows across six contexts: consular contact, travel, study, work, residence, and legal referral. Each row teaches a bounded German communication action and an Arabic verification instruction. It intentionally contains no visa eligibility rule, mandatory document list, legal deadline, compensation entitlement, official processing time, or guaranteed outcome.

All rows are:

- `officialClaimStatus: not-claimed`;
- `sourceRefs: []` until an authorized competent source is registered;
- `reviewStatus: specialist-review-pending`;
- mapped to a published A1–B2 lesson;
- owned by `practical-context-owner` and assigned to `legal-consular-specialist-reviewer-role`;
- bounded by `general-language-practice-not-legal-consular-or-eligibility-advice`.

Automated validation proves the registry shape, lesson mapping, six-context coverage, and absence of a fabricated official source. It does not replace actual review by a qualified consular/legal specialist. P1-377 remains partial until that final review is recorded.
